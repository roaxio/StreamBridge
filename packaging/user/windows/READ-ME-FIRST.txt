StreamBridge for Windows
========================
Use your phone (e.g. the EZ-Cam app) as this PC's camera and microphone.

1. Double-click StreamBridge.exe. No installation and no Python needed.
   Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC" because the app is not code-signed:
   click "More info" > "Run anyway". (You can verify the download, see the project page.)
2. The "Welcome / Setup check" window shows what is still missing, with links to the official pages:
   - OBS Studio 26+ (gives the camera "OBS Virtual Camera"): https://obsproject.com/download
   - VB-CABLE (gives the microphone "CABLE Output"):        https://vb-audio.com/Cable/
   Install them once, then click "Re-check". StreamBridge itself never installs anything.
3. Paste the video and/or audio URL your phone app shows, e.g. http://192.168.1.5:8080/video,
   and click Connect.
4. In Zoom/Teams/Discord/OBS/browser pick camera "OBS Virtual Camera" and microphone "CABLE Output".

Settings and logs: %APPDATA%\StreamBridge and %LOCALAPPDATA%\StreamBridge. "Reset to defaults" removes them.
To uninstall: delete StreamBridge.exe (and optionally the two folders above).
Help menu > "Welcome / Setup check" reopens the check at any time.
